6 Practical Checks Before Choosing a Web Design Company

Use this six-point checklist before signing off on any proposal for your small business:

  1. Inspect live client websites on mobile: Open two or three real examples on your phone to test loading speed, button sizing, and text clarity.
  2. Verify domain and hosting ownership: Confirm that your domain name and hosting account stay in your company's name with clear annual renewal costs.
  3. Check on-page SEO basics: Ask whether unique page titles, meta descriptions, clean URLs, image alt tags, and XML sitemaps are included by default.
  4. Confirm post-launch support terms: Clarify how minor text edits, backups, and security updates are handled once the website goes live.
  5. Review content structure early: Ensure the team plans headings and service explanations before jumping straight into decorative graphics.
  6. Ask about direct communication: Find out who actually builds your pages so feedback does not get lost across layers of sales reps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should a small business in Tiruvallur choose a static site or WordPress?

Static HTML websites are ultra-fast and low-maintenance for brochures that rarely change, while WordPress works best if you plan to publish regular blogs, offers, or product updates yourself.

Are SSL security certificates included when building a business website?

Yes. HTTPS SSL encryption is standard on modern hosting setups to protect visitor enquiries and prevent browser security warnings.

Can you redesign an outdated company website without losing our domain?

Yes. We build the refreshed website on a staging link first and switch it smoothly to your existing domain with minimal downtime and proper URL redirects.

Do small business websites include Google Search Console setup?

Yes. Submitting your sitemap to Google Search Console helps Google discover and index your pages faster after launch.

What ongoing annual costs should a small business expect?

Usually you only need domain renewal and web hosting renewal each year, plus optional monthly maintenance if you want hands-free updates and backups.
