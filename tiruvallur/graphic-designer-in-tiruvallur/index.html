7 Essentials for Sharp Logos, Flex Banners & Social Posts

Apply these seven practical standards across all your Tiruvallur brand assets:

  1. Scalable vector logos: Always build your logo in vector formats (AI, SVG, EPS, high-res PNG) so it looks razor-sharp on both a tiny visiting card and a 20-foot hoarding.
  2. Print-ready CMYK flex setup: Prepare banners and brochures in CMYK color mode with proper bleed margins and high DPI so local Tiruvallur print shops output true colors without cropping text.
  3. Clear three-second hierarchy: Highlight one bold offer or headline first, followed by one clear visual, and finish with your phone number and landmark.
  4. Maximum two font families: Pair one clean heading font with one readable body font—mixing five decorative scripts makes posters hard to read.
  5. Platform-correct social dimensions: Design dedicated sizes for Instagram square/portrait posts, WhatsApp Status/Reels covers (1080×1920), and Facebook headers so nothing gets cut off.
  6. High contrast for outdoor readability: Ensure strong foreground-to-background contrast so flex boards stay legible under bright Tamil Nadu afternoon sunlight.
  7. Reusable brand kit: Maintain a one-page sheet with your exact brand color hex codes, fonts, and logo variations for every future campaign.
