Common Google Maps Listing Problems Cuddalore Owners Face

Before creating a brand-new listing, it pays to check what already exists on Google Maps. Across Cuddalore district, three recurring problems quietly hurt local businesses:

  • Duplicate listings: One unclaimed listing created years ago by a visitor alongside a second partial listing created by staff—splitting reviews and confusing Google Maps.
  • Wrong map pin placement: Customers tapping "Directions" get routed to the back lane or an adjacent street instead of your main storefront entrance.
  • Verification rejections or suspensions: Mismatched signage names, virtual office addresses, or repeated rapid edits to critical fields can trigger sudden verification loops.

6 Compliant Steps for Setting Up a Google Maps Listing in Cuddalore

Follow these six clean, guideline-friendly steps to establish a rock-solid listing:

  1. Audit existing Map pins first: Search your business name, old phone number, and street address on Google Maps to claim an existing verified pin instead of creating an accidental duplicate.
  2. Prepare clear verification proof: Have permanent exterior signage photos, valid Indian business registration documents (such as Udyam, GST, or shop license), and an active business phone ready before starting verification.
  3. Enter exact, formatted address & pin coordinates: Match your address cleanly to recognizable Cuddalore landmarks and verify the exact street entrance pin in Satellite View.
  4. Configure primary ownership securely: Always set the business owner's own permanent Google account as Primary Owner—never hand exclusive ownership to a temporary third party.
  5. Complete every profile detail carefully: Fill in business description, opening date, operating hours, accurate service categories, real storefront photos, and your linked website URL.
  6. Monitor suggested edits periodically: Because public Map users or automated systems occasionally suggest incorrect hours or closures, checking your dashboard monthly keeps your live listing accurate.
