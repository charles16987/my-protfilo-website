7 Practical Graphic Design Tips for Logos, Posters &amp; Social Media

Whether you are launching a fresh Cuddalore brand or refreshing seasonal promotions, apply these seven crisp design principles:

  1. Simple, scalable logo marks: Design a clean vector logo that looks sharp equally on a huge storefront board, a tiny Instagram profile circle, a WhatsApp avatar, and a black-and-white invoice stamp.
  2. One clear headline hierarchy: Every poster or social graphic should have one unmistakable focal headline, one concise benefit or offer, and one clear Call or WhatsApp action—never cram a whole catalog onto a single square.
  3. Strict two-font discipline: Pair one strong display typeface for headlines with one clean sans-serif font for supporting details to keep Tamil Nadu bilingual or English creatives crisp and legible.
  4. High contrast for small phone screens: Remember that most social posters are viewed on a bright 6-inch smartphone outdoors—generous padding and sharp text contrast keep details readable without zooming.
  5. Platform-exact dimensions: Export dedicated ratios for Instagram square posts, vertical WhatsApp Status / Reels covers, YouTube thumbnails, and print-ready CMYK PDFs for local flex and brochure printers.
  6. Real product or business photography: Frame authentic photos of your actual dishes, sarees, cashew tins, clinic equipment, or completed interiors neatly inside clean layouts instead of generic clip art.
  7. Editable brand kit files on handover: Keep master vector logo files (SVG/PDF/PNG), exact hex color codes, and font names saved cleanly so every future banner stays visually unified.
