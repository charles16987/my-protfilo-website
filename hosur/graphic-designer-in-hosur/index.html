Making Sure You Receive Proper Print and Digital Source Files

CMYK Bleed Files for Print vs. RGB Web Files

Colors that look vibrant on a phone screen can print dull if exported incorrectly. Always confirm your designer delivers CMYK PDFs with proper 3mm bleed margins for printers alongside lightweight RGB PNG or WebP images for WhatsApp, Instagram, and websites.

Readable Hierarchy on Small Mobile Screens

Most social posts are viewed on a six-inch smartphone screen for less than two seconds. Focusing each graphic on one bold headline, one clear product or service visual, and one concise contact detail performs far better than paragraphs of tiny text.
