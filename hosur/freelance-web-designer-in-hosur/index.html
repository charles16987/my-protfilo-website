4 Things to Check Before Hiring Any Freelance Web Designer

Review Live Portfolio Sites on Your Own Smartphone

Do not judge solely by static desktop mockups. Open live client websites from the designer's portfolio right on your phone over mobile data. Check how fast pages load, whether menus tap cleanly, and whether contact buttons are immediately easy to find.

Confirm Scope, Timeline, and Post-Launch Ownership in Writing

A professional freelancer always shares a clear, plain-English outline before starting: exact page count, included features (forms, maps, SSL, on-page SEO), revision milestones, payment schedule, and post-launch care options like website maintenance plans in Hosur.

What a Smooth Freelance Website Process Looks Like

A clear four-step workflow keeps every project organized and predictable:

  1. Brief & Goal Alignment: Share your business profile, target customers in Hosur, sample sites you admire, and desired enquiry actions.
  2. Layout Preview & Feedback: Review the homepage and key service layout early so visual style and messaging match your brand cleanly.
  3. Responsive Build & Testing: Build out all pages, compress imagery, set up on-page SEO tags and schema, and test every link on mobile and desktop.
  4. Launch & Handover: Connect your custom domain, verify HTTPS security and Google Search Console indexing, and hand over full access.
