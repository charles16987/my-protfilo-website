7 Practical Steps a Shopify Expert Follows to Launch Your Store

Launching smoothly with a Shopify expert in Madurai follows seven clear, structured stages:

  1. 1. Catalog & collection taxonomy: Structure clear categories—such as Cotton Sarees, Festive Silk, New Arrivals, or Under ₹1,500—with helpful filters so shoppers browse effortlessly.
  2. 2. Branded mobile-first theme customization: Customize a fast Online Store 2.0 theme to showcase crisp imagery, readable typography, and sticky mobile Add-to-Cart visibility.
  3. 3. Complete product setup & variants: Configure SKU codes, colors, sizes, fabric or material details, wash care notes, and inventory tracking accurately.
  4. 4. Indian UPI & payment gateway setup: Connect Razorpay, Cashfree, or PayU for smooth UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), card checkout, and optional Cash-on-Delivery controls.
  5. 5. Automated courier & shipping zones: Configure Shiprocket or Delhivery rates by weight and region so dispatch labels and customer tracking links generate smoothly.
  6. 6. Trust & policy pages: Publish clear Shipping, Exchange/Return, Privacy, Contact, and About Us pages to earn instant buyer confidence and smooth gateway approval.
  7. 7. Real mobile test order before go-live: Verify full checkout flow, confirmation email, WhatsApp order support icon, and GST invoice clarity on real smartphones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you set up a Shopify store if I have never sold online before?

Yes. Everything from theme design and initial product uploads to UPI gateway setup, shipping rates, and a simple order-handling walkthrough is included.

Does Shopify support Indian Rupees (INR), GST display, and UPI payments?

Yes. Stores operate natively in INR, integrate smoothly with Razorpay or Cashfree for UPI and cards, and support GST-compliant invoice templates.

How many products are uploaded during initial store setup?

Most retailers launch cleanly with 15 to 40 core products configured as part of setup, and you receive clear guidance to add unlimited future items effortlessly.

Can customers still message us on WhatsApp if they have questions before buying?

Yes. A clean WhatsApp support button can sit neatly on product pages so shoppers can ask about colors, sizing, or bulk festive orders immediately.

Will I need a developer every time I want to run a Pongal or Deepavali discount?

No. Shopify's built-in discount manager lets you create percentage offers, free-shipping thresholds, or coupon codes in less than a minute.
