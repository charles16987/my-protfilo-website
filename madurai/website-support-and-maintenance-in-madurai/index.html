7 Essential Tasks in Website Support and Maintenance in Madurai

Consistent monthly care prevents emergencies through seven straightforward technical checks:

  1. 1. Automated off-site cloud backups: Keep clean restorable snapshots of your files and database stored safely off your primary server.
  2. 2. Careful core, theme & plugin updates: Update WordPress, WooCommerce, or libraries only after taking a fresh backup and verifying layout stability right afterward.
  3. 3. Malware scanning & security hardening: Monitor file integrity, block brute-force login bots, and patch known vulnerabilities promptly.
  4. 4. SSL certificate & domain expiry checks: Prevent embarrassing 'Not Secure' browser warnings or accidental domain lapses.
  5. 5. Rapid bug & error resolution: Fix 500 server errors, broken mobile menus, layout shifts, or missing images swiftly before customers encounter them.
  6. 6. Speed, caching & broken-link audits: Keep page loading crisp on mobile data, trim bloated database logs, and repair dead internal links.
  7. 7. Routine content & banner updates: Swap festival offer banners, add new staff profiles, update phone numbers, or upload fresh gallery photos whenever your business changes.

5 Common Website Problems That Should Never Wait

If you spot any of these issues on your Madurai website today, resolve them immediately:

  • Contact forms not sending enquiries: Often caused by unauthenticated server mail rules fixed cleanly with proper SMTP setup.
  • 'Your connection is not private' SSL alert: Usually an expired certificate or mixed HTTP/HTTPS image link.
  • White screen or WordPress critical error: Typically triggered by a conflicting plugin update or outdated PHP version.
  • Broken checkout or payment callback errors: Requires immediate testing of gateway webhooks and currency settings.
  • Sluggish 8-second mobile load times: Usually resolved by image compression, clean caching, and trimming heavy third-party widgets.
