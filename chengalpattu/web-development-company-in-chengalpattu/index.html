6 Technical Priorities When Evaluating a Web Development Partner

Measure any proposed development partner against these six practical engineering checks:

  1. Right-sized technology stack: Choose the simplest reliable stack for the job—static HTML/React for ultra-fast marketing sites, WordPress for editorial CMS, or Shopify/WooCommerce for stores.
  2. Core Web Vitals performance: Verify lightweight bundles, deferred non-critical scripts, and responsive image sizing so landing pages score cleanly on speed checks.
  3. Clean URL & SEO architecture: Ensure semantic headings, structured JSON-LD schema, canonical URLs, and clean routing are engineered in—not patched on after launch.
  4. Reliable lead & CRM routing: Test form validation, spam protection, SMTP delivery, and WhatsApp or email alerts thoroughly before going live.
  5. Full source & infrastructure ownership: Confirm you hold full access to domains, hosting panels, and code repositories without vendor lock-in.
  6. Clear staging & handover process: Insist on reviewing builds on a staging URL and receiving clean documentation for post-launch edits.
