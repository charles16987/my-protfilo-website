7 Graphic Design Essentials for Logos, Banners & Brochures

Hold every brand asset to these seven practical design standards:

  1. Timeless vector logo design: Craft a simple, recognizable mark that stays crisp in single-color stamps, embroidery, website headers, and large outdoor boards.
  2. Print-accurate CMYK & bleed setup: Supply tri-fold brochures, flyers, and standees in 300 DPI CMYK PDF format with proper 3mm bleed so local printers trim cleanly.
  3. Scannable brochure structure: Organize brochures with clear panels—Cover Hook, About & Strengths, Core Services/Products, and Contact Details with QR code.
  4. Disciplined color palette: Stick to 1 primary brand color, 1 accent color, and clean neutrals so layouts look intentional rather than chaotic.
  5. Legible font pairing: Combine one strong heading typeface with a crisp sans-serif body font kept large enough for comfortable reading.
  6. Sharp original visuals: Pair real, color-corrected photographs of your shop, campus, or products with clean custom icons.
  7. Consistent social media templates: Reuse branded header/footer frames for Instagram posts, WhatsApp flyers, and LinkedIn company updates.
