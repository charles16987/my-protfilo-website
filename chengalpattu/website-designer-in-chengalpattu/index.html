7 Standards of a Modern Mobile-Friendly Website

Check your website design against these seven practical standards before launch:

  1. Readable 16px+ body typography: Ensure paragraphs and bullet points read comfortably outdoors without squinting or horizontal scrolling.
  2. Thumb-friendly buttons & links: Space out menus, Call buttons, and WhatsApp links so visitors never mis-tap neighbouring items.
  3. Compressed next-gen imagery: Resize hero photos and galleries accurately so pages load swiftly even on patchy highway mobile data.
  4. Scannable section rhythm: Break text into concise paragraphs, crisp H2 headings, and clean service cards so busy readers grasp your offer in ten seconds.
  5. Instant navigation drawer: Keep the top header slim with a clean slide-out menu and one prominent Contact button.
  6. Short, mobile-friendly enquiry forms: Use numeric keypads for phone inputs and keep required fields to three or four essentials.
  7. Clear local trust cues: Show your Chengalpattu address, service areas, hours, and real business photos clearly near the bottom and contact page.
