6 Essentials Every Kanchipuram Silk & Retail Website Needs

Ensure your web design partner executes these six brand-critical elements cleanly:

  1. True-to-life high-clarity photography: Optimize full-drape, pallu, and zari-border zoom images in modern WebP so fine textures stay sharp without sluggish load times.
  2. Clear collection & occasion sorting: Organize collections naturally by Bridal Muhurtham, Pure Zari Kanjivaram, Korvai, Festive Silk, and Gift Sarees.
  3. Authenticity & heritage storytelling: Highlight handloom lineage, pure mulberry silk sourcing, zari craftsmanship, and genuine Silk Mark certification transparently.
  4. Video-shopping appointment booking: Offer an effortless WhatsApp or calendar booking button for NRI and out-of-city families wanting live showroom drape previews.
  5. Consistent showroom & online branding: Harmonize typography, subtle gold/jewel accents, and clean white/dark spacing so products pop without visual clutter.
  6. Clean SEO & ownership foundation: Guarantee full domain ownership, clean schema markup, and fast mobile speeds for organic discovery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a silk showroom start with a lookbook website before adding online checkout?

Yes. Many Kanchipuram silk brands launch first with a fast catalogue and WhatsApp video-shopping appointment flow, then add Shopify or WooCommerce checkout smoothly later.

How do you keep high-resolution saree photos from slowing down the website?

Images are sized accurately, compressed into crisp WebP formats, and lazy-loaded below the fold so fine zari details stay sharp while pages remain fast.

Can NRI and international wedding shoppers use our website smoothly?

Yes. Fast global CDN hosting, clear WhatsApp video-call booking, and optional multi-currency Shopify checkout make overseas shopping effortless.

Will our staff be able to upload fresh wedding season collections easily?

Yes. Building on WordPress or Shopify lets your showroom team upload newly woven sarees and mark sold pieces out-of-stock in minutes.

Do we retain full ownership of our website domain and brand assets?

Yes. Your domain, hosting, and all website assets stay 100% under your brand's ownership.
