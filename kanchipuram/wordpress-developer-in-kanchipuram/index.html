7 Essentials of a Clean WordPress Business & Catalogue Build

Insist on these seven build standards so your WordPress site stays fast and trouble-free:

  1. Structured custom categories & filters: Organize products cleanly by occasion, color, fabric, or industrial category so shoppers narrow choices quickly.
  2. Automatic SKU/item-name WhatsApp links: Pre-fill the product title or code inside the WhatsApp chat message so staff know instantly which item the customer wants.
  3. Automated image compression & WebP: Keep multi-photo catalogue pages light even when uploading dozens of new product shots each month.
  4. Lean theme & child-theme coding: Avoid clunky multipurpose themes loaded with unused sliders that break during WordPress updates.
  5. Clean URL & on-page SEO hierarchy: Ensure every product category and service page gets readable slugs, meta tags, and XML sitemap inclusion.
  6. Security & scheduled off-site backups: Lock down admin login pages, enable SSL, and run automatic weekly cloud backups.
  7. Simple staff editing guide: Receive a clear step-by-step walkthrough so adding a new catalogue item takes under two minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we hide prices on a WordPress catalogue and show 'Enquire on WhatsApp' instead?

Yes. Catalogue mode is ideal for custom silk weaves, jewellery, or wholesale goods where prices depend on weight, zari purity, or order quantity.

How many products or photos can a WordPress catalogue handle smoothly?

With proper image optimization and clean hosting, WordPress comfortably handles hundreds of catalogue entries without slowing down.

Will I need a developer every time I want to add a new saree or product?

No. You get an intuitive upload screen and quick training so your own staff can add, edit, or mark items sold anytime.

Can you convert an enquiry catalogue into a full online store later?

Yes. Enabling WooCommerce checkout with Razorpay UPI and courier shipping builds directly on top of your existing catalog.

How do we keep a WordPress site safe from hacking or spam?

Strong passwords, minimal trusted plugins, SSL encryption, spam-filtered forms, and scheduled monthly updates and backups keep WordPress secure.
