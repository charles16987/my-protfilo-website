7 Ethical Steps to Improve Your Google Visibility

Steady organic growth comes from executing these seven core SEO steps cleanly:

  1. Real buyer keyword research: Uncover exact product, service, and regional phrases people type across Kanchipuram and India—avoiding vague, low-intent vanity terms.
  2. Technical crawl & speed cleanup: Fix slow images, broken internal links, duplicate pages, missing canonical tags, and mobile usability errors reported in Search Console.
  3. Dedicated intent-matched pages: Create focused pages for each core service, weave category, or room type instead of burying everything on one crowded homepage.
  4. Clear titles, headings & helpful copy: Write engaging titles, crisp H2/H3 outlines, and genuinely informative answers about quality, pricing factors, and processes.
  5. Clean structured schema markup: Implement accurate BreadcrumbList, Product, Article, LocalBusiness, and FAQ schema cleanly.
  6. Thoughtful internal linking: Guide both Google and readers naturally from regional hubs and educational guides straight to your core service or store pages.
  7. Monthly Search Console review: Monitor impressions, clicks, and emerging queries monthly to refine pages that sit close to page one.
