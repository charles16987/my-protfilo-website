7 Design Essentials for Logos, Packaging & Social Creatives

Keep your brand looking polished across print and digital by applying these seven principles:

  1. Scalable foil-friendly vector logo: Design clean vector marks that reproduce crisply in gold foil stamping on saree boxes, woven fabric tags, and small Instagram avatars.
  2. Accurate printer dielines & bleed: Prepare rigid boxes, sweet cartons, and paper bags to exact printer cut/fold dimensions in 300 DPI CMYK so nothing misaligns on press.
  3. Refined color restraint: Pair one rich signature brand color (such as deep maroon, temple gold, peacock teal, or ivory) with clean neutral backgrounds so products stay the star.
  4. Clear care & authenticity inserts: Include a tidy Silk Care & Thank-You card inside every box featuring your website URL and WhatsApp QR code for effortless reorders.
  5. Scroll-stopping social layouts: Frame saree drapes, dishes, or retail offers cleanly with minimal text overlays—never cover gorgeous products with messy starburst stickers.
  6. Consistent bilingual typography: Balance English and Tamil brand text harmoniously when designing local flyers, festival wishes, and showroom boards.
  7. Organized brand style sheet: Save approved fonts, hex/CMYK color codes, and logo lockups so every future designer or printer stays 100% consistent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you prepare packaging files ready for gold foiling or spot UV printing?

Yes. Separate vector foil/spot-UV mask layers and CMYK print PDFs can be supplied directly according to your box printer's dieline template.

What items can be included in a complete showroom branding kit?

A typical kit includes a scalable vector logo, visiting card, saree box/carry bag artwork, product hangtag, thank-you/care card, and social media templates.

Do you also create monthly Instagram and WhatsApp promotional posts?

Yes. Monthly creative packs keep your brand colors, fonts, and festive announcements consistent without daily design stress.

Can you update or modernize an old family business logo without losing heritage recognition?

Yes. A gentle logo refinement cleans up rough lines and typography for modern screens and print while preserving the familiar heritage symbol customers trust.

How long does packaging or logo design usually take?

Logo concepts typically arrive within 2 to 4 working days, while custom box packaging layouts take 3 to 5 working days once exact box dimensions are shared.
