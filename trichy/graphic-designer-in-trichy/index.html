How to Prepare a Quick Design Brief

Sharing four quick details upfront helps your graphic designer in Trichy nail the concept faster:

  • Purpose and size: Clarify whether the artwork is for Instagram, WhatsApp status, A4 brochure print, or outdoor hoarding.
  • Exact headline and contact text: Double-check spelling, phone numbers, offer validity, and address lines before layout starts.
  • Brand mood and color preferences: Mention whether your brand leans modern minimal, traditional festive, medical clean, or bold retail.
  • High-resolution product or store photos: Clean source photos elevate final posters far better than heavily compressed forwarded images.
