6 Steps to Build and Launch Your Shopify Store Smoothly

Launching a dependable online storefront follows six structured stages rather than rushing products onto a default template:

  1. Store structure and collections: Organize products into clear collections, tags, and filterable attributes (size, fabric, weight, or price) so shoppers find items fast.
  2. Mobile-first theme customization: Customize a fast Online Store 2.0 theme to match your brand identity, clean typography, and prominent Add to Cart buttons.
  3. Product page conversion details: Include sharp multi-angle photos, clear sizing or usage notes, delivery estimates, and transparent return policies.
  4. Indian payment gateway setup: Integrate trusted gateways such as Razorpay, Cashfree, or PhonePe to support UPI, Google Pay, debit/credit cards, netbanking, and Cash on Delivery rules.
  5. Automated shipping and GST configuration: Connect aggregators like Shiprocket or Delhivery for automated pincode serviceability, label printing, and tracking SMS updates, alongside GST invoice settings.
  6. Pre-launch test orders: Run real end-to-end test checkouts on mobile to verify discounts, confirmation emails, and shipping rate calculations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Shopify support Indian Rupees, UPI, and Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Shopify supports INR pricing, Cash on Delivery (COD), and major Indian payment gateways like Razorpay and Cashfree for UPI, Google Pay, cards, and netbanking.

How long does it take to set up a complete Shopify store?

A well-organized Shopify store with theme customization, initial product uploads, payments, and shipping rules typically takes 10 to 15 working days.

Can I manage orders and add new products from my mobile phone?

Yes. The official Shopify mobile app lets you add products, edit stock levels, receive instant order notifications, and fulfill shipments right from your phone.

Can you customize my existing Shopify store without rebuilding everything?

Yes. Existing stores can be improved with cleaner product page layouts, Liquid customizations, speed cleanups, and checkout or shipping refinements.

Do I need GST registration to sell on Shopify in India?

Requirements depend on your business category and interstate selling plans. Consult your chartered accountant for compliance advice, and we can configure your store's tax and invoice settings accordingly.
