The Cost of Neglecting Website Maintenance

Unmaintained websites become easy targets for automated cyberattacks. Vulnerabilities in outdated software packages allow malicious bots to inject phishing redirects, steal customer contact inquiries, or deface company domains. Once Google detects malware on a domain, it flags the site with a severe warning ("This site may be hacked"), causing immediate traffic collapses and brand reputation damage.

By following security guidelines from the OWASP Foundation, our engineering practices keep your code resilient against common exploits such as SQL injection and Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).
