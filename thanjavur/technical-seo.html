Our Core Technical SEO Solutions

1. Core Web Vitals & Speed Optimization

Google's Core Web Vitals (LCP, FID, CLS) are now major ranking factors. I optimize your server response times, minify code, and compress assets to ensure your site passes the "PageSpeed Insights" test with flying colors.

2. Structured Data & Schema Markup

I implement advanced JSON-LD Schema markup for local businesses, products, FAQs, and reviews. This helps your site appear as a "Rich Snippet" in search results, significantly increasing your click-through rate (CTR).

3. Crawlability & Indexing Audit

I perform deep audits to find and fix 404 errors, broken redirects, and crawl budget issues. By optimizing your internal linking and XML sitemaps, I make it easy for Googlebot to navigate your site.

4. Mobile-First & Security (HTTPS)

I ensure your site is perfectly responsive and secure. From SSL certificate installation to fixing mobile usability issues in Search Console, I cover all bases.
