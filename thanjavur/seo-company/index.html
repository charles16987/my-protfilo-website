5 Metrics That Guarantee Commercial ROI

We gauge campaign success on five verifiable business metrics:

  • 1. Commercial Intent Keyword Positions: Tracking rankings for high-value transactional queries (e.g., "hire civil engineer Thanjavur", "buy Tanjore paintings online") that directly drive revenue.
  • 2. Organic Lead Conversion Rate: Measuring the percentage of search visitors who complete a contact inquiry, request a callback, or place an order.
  • 3. Cost Per Organic Acquisition (CPA): Lowering your blended customer acquisition cost by compounding organic search traffic over time compared to paid Google Ads.
  • 4. Organic Click-Through Rate (CTR): Crafting compelling title tags and persuasive meta descriptions to ensure searchers choose your listing over neighboring results.
  • 5. Average Time on Page & Scroll Depth: Ensuring visitors consume your comprehensive content and trust your authority rather than bouncing immediately.
