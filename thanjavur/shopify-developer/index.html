Purging Ghost App Scripts & Speed Audits

When you uninstall apps from Shopify, they frequently leave behind "ghost scripts"—leftover snippets of JavaScript in your theme.liquid file that continue firing tracking calls on every customer visit. We conduct comprehensive theme audits: isolating and deleting orphaned scripts, minifying custom CSS, and implementing lazy-loading to reclaim critical speed.
