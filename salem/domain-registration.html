How to Choose the Right Domain Name for Your Salem Business

Picking the right domain name is both an art and a strategy. Here are the principles we follow when helping Salem clients choose their domain:

✔ Keep It Short and Simple: Shorter domain names are easier to type, remember, and share. Aim for under 15 characters where possible.
✔ Reflect Your Brand: Your domain should closely match your business name or the core service you offer in Salem.
✔ Avoid Numbers and Hyphens: These create confusion when telling someone your web address verbally.
✔ Think About Keywords: Including a relevant keyword like your service or location can naturally support your SEO visibility in local searches.
✔ Check Social Media Availability: A consistent name across your domain and social media handles strengthens your brand identity.
