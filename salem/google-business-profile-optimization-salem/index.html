7 Practical Steps to Strengthen Your Presence on Google Maps

Google evaluates local map visibility largely through relevance, distance, and prominence. While distance depends on where the searcher stands, you can directly improve relevance and prominence through these seven steps:

  1. Exact, policy-compliant business name: Match your official shop board or company registration verbatim—never pack extra city names or keyword strings into the title.
  2. Precise primary and secondary categories: Select the exact primary category that defines your core business, then add relevant secondary categories only where accurate.
  3. Verified address and exact entrance pin: Zoom closely on the map editor so navigation drops visitors cleanly at your street entrance around Fairlands, Gugai, Shevapet, or Suramangalam.
  4. Complete service and product entries: List core offerings with clear short descriptions so Google understands every specific solution you provide.
  5. Current regular and holiday hours: Update festival hours ahead of Tamil Nadu holidays so customers trust your Open Now status.
  6. Authentic exterior, interior, and work photos: Upload crisp, well-lit daytime photos showing your signage, reception or shelves, and real products or completed work.
  7. Active review responses and periodic updates: Thank reviewers genuinely and publish occasional offer or announcement posts to signal an active business.

Small Profile Details That Increase Calls and Visits

Once core details are accurate, three small additions make a noticeable difference in customer confidence:

  • Populate common Q&A entries: Answer frequent questions about parking availability, appointment booking, home delivery, or UPI payment options clearly.
  • Set accurate business attributes: Mark helpful details such as wheelchair accessibility, women-owned status, on-site services, or online appointments where applicable.
  • Share a direct review QR code at billing: Encouraging genuine feedback from happy local buyers steadily builds prominence over time.
