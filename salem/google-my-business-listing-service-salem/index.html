6 Essentials Included in a Clean Google My Business Listing Setup

Setting up a listing correctly from the beginning saves weeks of back-and-forth troubleshooting:

  1. Duplicate listing check first: Before creating a new profile, check whether Google Maps already has an unclaimed or older user-generated pin for your address so you do not create conflicting duplicates.
  2. Clean ownership setup under your Google account: Always keep primary ownership under a secure business Google account you control directly—never let an external party lock you out.
  3. Policy-safe naming and address formatting: Enter your exact signage name and standardize your Salem street address, landmark, and pin code cleanly.
  4. Verification readiness checklist: Prepare permanent outdoor signage, interior workspace cues, and supporting business registration documents if Google requests video verification.
  5. Complete service, product, and description entries: Write clear, helpful summaries of what you offer alongside realistic business hours and website/phone links.
  6. Starter photo library & review link: Upload clean real-world photos and generate your short direct Google review URL for sharing with happy customers.
